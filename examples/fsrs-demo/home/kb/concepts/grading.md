## Explanation

FSRS learns from your grades, so it trusts their meaning: **Again** = forgot, **Hard/Good/Easy** = recalled. Pressing Hard when you actually forgot teaches the model that you remembered, which inflates stability and makes later intervals unreasonably long. It is the one habit the Anki manual says FSRS cannot adapt to.
