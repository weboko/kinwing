result: "Three per-card variables: R (recall probability now), S (days until R hits 90%), D (how hard it is to raise S) [cl-20261028-6qtn]. R decays daily along the forgetting curve; S and D change only at reviews. A success multiplies S by a factor that is smaller for high D and high S and larger when R was low [cl-20261028-frr9]; a lapse uses a separate post-lapse formula."
